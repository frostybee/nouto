Deleting a collection, folder, or saved request moves it to **Trash** instead of removing it immediately. The **Trash** tab in the sidebar shows a badge with the number of items it holds.

## Restore an item

Open the **Trash** tab and click **Restore** beside the item. Each item shows its original location as `Collection / Folder` and how long ago you deleted it.

Nouto puts the item back where it was:

- A collection returns to the sidebar.
- A folder or request returns to its original folder, at its original position.
- If the original folder no longer exists, the item goes to the root of its collection.
- If the original collection no longer exists, the item goes to **Drafts**.

## Permanently delete items

Click **Delete permanently** beside an item, or click **Empty Trash** in the Trash toolbar to remove every item. Both actions ask for confirmation and can't be undone.

## Trash limits

Nouto removes items from Trash automatically:

- Items older than 30 days are deleted when Nouto loads your data.
- Trash holds up to 100 items. Deleting another item removes the oldest one.

Trash holds collection content only. Request history has its own delete and clear controls. See [Request history](/tools/request-history).
